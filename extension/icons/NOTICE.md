# Icon credit

The icons in this folder are based on AboveVTT's icon (`icon.png` in the AboveVTT extension):

- `icon16.png`, `icon32.png`, `icon48.png`, `icon128.png`: AboveVTT's icon, resized. Nothing else was changed.
- `status/*.png` (the toolbar icons): modified versions. The red "A" is made smaller and the letters "MCP" are
  drawn underneath in a colour that shows the connection state. Made by `tools/make_status_icons.py`.
